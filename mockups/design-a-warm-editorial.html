What happened

Torrential rain, flash floods, and neighbours left to recover.

On November 4, 2025, the city of Valencia experienced a severe weather event that brought torrential rain, flash floods, and widespread damage across urban and rural areas. The storm disrupted transportation, flooded homes, and affected essential infrastructure.

The most affected areas include the municipalities of Torrent, Alzira, and Carcaixent. Rescue and recovery operations remain ongoing as local communities work to restore normal conditions.

How it works

Small tasks, finished, add up.

  1. 1Post a task

    One concrete need, with a place on the map.

  2. 2A volunteer offers

    Someone nearby sends you a message.

  3. 3You accept

    You decide, then agree on the details.

  4. 4Mark it done

    So help can move on to the next street.

Match is a free, volunteer-run platform that connects people affected by the DANA with neighbours ready to help.
